Installer, branding, and a binary-name fix

- packaging/forgeset.toml: the Voicekit installer (Forgeset), validated
  with `forgeset validate` and visually checked with `forgeset preview`.
  Fluent (Windows 11) template, dark theme, the app's own accent colour
  (#7a8cff) -- sleek and professional, no mascot template, no file-type
  associations or library page (Voicekit has no player/indexer). Recommended
  components: dictation (whisper-base), Speak for me (tts-kokoro), voice
  cloning (tts-chatterbox), optional NVIDIA TensorRT for RTX.
- build.ps1: stages voicekit.exe + voicekit-studio.exe, the app-local VC++
  runtime, DirectML, and the recommended models, then runs Forgeset.
- packaging/gen_icon.py: replaced the cat-mascot icon the app scaffold had
  copied in from Nekotone with a plain waveform mark on the app's own
  dark/indigo gradient, used by both the app window and the installer.
- Renamed the Tauri app's binary from `voicekit` to `voicekit-studio`: it
  was colliding with the CLI's `voicekit` binary name, which would have
  overwritten one with the other when both land in the installer's
  {app}/bin. Verified with a clean `cargo check` on src-tauri afterward.
- HANDOFF.md/CHANGELOG.md updated to match.
